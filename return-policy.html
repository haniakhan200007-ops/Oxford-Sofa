Return Policy

1. Return Eligibility

Customers who wish to return a sofa should contact our team to discuss their request. Returns are subject to the applicable return policy and approval.

2. Damaged or Defective Products

If your sofa arrives damaged or defective, please contact our customer support team as soon as possible. Share photos of the issue so our team can review your request and assist you.

3. Exchange Policy

Customers who wish to exchange a sofa should contact our team for assistance. Exchanges are subject to product availability and the applicable exchange conditions.

4. How to Request a Return

To request a return, contact our customer support team and provide your order details along with the reason for your request. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

5. Refund Information

Refund eligibility and processing depend on the outcome of the return request and the applicable policy. Please contact our team for information about your refund.
